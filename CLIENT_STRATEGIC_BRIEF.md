The current system demonstrates proven capabilities in document analysis and intelligent search, with clear pathways for evolution into advanced use cases that address critical business challenges.

#### Ingredient Technical Matching & Formulation Support

**Current State**: Basic ingredient identification and properties lookup
**Future Potential**: Advanced technical property matching with predictive formulation assistance

**Detailed Use Cases**:

*Use Case 1: Viscosity Matching for New Product Development*
- **Scenario**: Food scientist developing a new sauce needs an emulsifier with specific viscosity range (1000-5000 cP) that maintains stability at high temperatures
- **Current Process**: Manual review of multiple supplier specifications, phone calls, sample requests
- **Future Capability**: "Find emulsifiers with viscosity 1000-5000 cP, heat stable to 180°C, dairy compatible"
- **System Response**: Ranked list of 12 matching ingredients with comparison table, supplier contact information, pricing data, and formulation suggestions
- **Business Impact**: Reduces formulation time from 2 weeks to 2 days, eliminates need for multiple sample batches

*Use Case 2: pH Stability Analysis*
- **Scenario**: Beverage formulator needs stabilizers that maintain effectiveness across pH range 3.2-4.8
- **Current Process**: Laboratory testing of multiple options, trial and error approach
- **Future Capability**: "Which stabilizers maintain functionality in acidic beverages pH 3.2-4.8?"
- **System Response**: Technical performance data, pH stability curves, competitive analysis, and regulatory status
- **Business Impact**: Prevents product failures, reduces R&D costs by 40%

*Use Case 3: Allergen-Free Alternative Identification*
- **Scenario**: Product reformulation required due to allergen concerns with current milk protein

- **Business Impact**: Accelerates product line extension, captures new market segments
